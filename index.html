CV

I am a PhD Candidate in Philosophy at Columbia University. I work in the history of philosophy and ethics. My BA was in Philosophy at King’s College London, and I did an MPhil in Intellectual History at the University of Cambridge. For the 2025-2026 academic year I was a DAAD research fellow at the University of Potsdam.

My dissertation is on the concept of evil in Kant’s and Hegel’s accounts of rational agency, and what contemporary moral psychology and ethics might learn from it.

Vices I am currently thinking about include hypocrisy, conceitedness, and miserliness.
